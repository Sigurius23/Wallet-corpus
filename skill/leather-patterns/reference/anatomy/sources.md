## C. Explicitly discounted

| Source | Why not cited for a number |
|---|---|
| Forum thickness figures (backbone 4.5 mm+, belly 0.8–3 mm) | unsplit hide, one person's experience; splitting makes them moot |
| Retailer "quality" tables (best/better/fair) | market value ranking, not a physical measurement |
| "Shell is a flat muscle / panniculus carnosus" | retracted by its own proposer; contradicted by the histology |
| Grokipedia "Hide (skin)" numbers (194.2 µm back vs 150.3 µm abdomen; 15–30 MPa corium) | AI-generated aggregator with uncited attributions — figures may be traceable upstream, but **do not cite the aggregator**; cited as ~ only |
| Any single-study claim about follicle density | the bovine-hide source itself calls its own numbers "informed extrapolation" |

## D. Irreducibly uncertain — carried openly, not fixed

1. **Culatta.** Two incompatible definitions, both from sources that state them
   confidently. Resolved by *procedure* (demand a diagram + species), not by finding the
   "right" answer.
2. **Whether the butt is the strongest region.** Two surveys disagree; one says no region
   is consistently strongest, the other puts peak tensile in the flank. Resolved by
   *reframing* the claim to uniformity.
3. **Which way the fibres run.** "Parallel to backbone" (textbook), "perpendicular"
   (SAXS), "rotates across the hide away from the OSP", "lengthwise in the rump but
   crosswise in butt and neck" (one retailer). Four statements, four length scales or
   four regions. Do not resolve — instruct the reader to measure.
4. **Follicle density / depth for bovine specifically.** Available figures are
   extrapolated and their own author says so.
5. **Shell cordovan's exact histological identity.** "Hyaline layer" is the best
   supported term; the muscle reading is retracted but still circulates widely. Fine for
   design decisions (no grain, rolls, dense) — not fine as an authoritative anatomy.

## H. Irreducibly uncertain in this pass

1. **The ostrich foot anatomy** rests on **one** natural-history source. It is detailed and
   internally coherent, and it is not contradicted anywhere, but it is one source. The
   "two toes / hoof-like nail / no claw on toe 2" structure is textbook ornithology and
   should be checkable against any ostrich anatomy reference — worth a second read before
   publication.
2. **Ostrich crown area** (1/3 of skin; 5–6 sq ft of ~16) is supplier consensus, not a
   standard. The *proportion* is quoted everywhere and is probably right; the square footage
   is not.
3. **Crocodilian grade → value percentages** (~100/75/50 %) come from one trade article. The
   *direction* is obviously right; the numbers are one person's rule of thumb.
4. **Goat region → strength** is a single well-powered study (Iranian goats). It contradicts
   the bovine folk rule, which is exactly why it is valuable, but it has not been replicated
   across goat breeds in the sources found. Do not present it as universal; present it as
   "the one properly-powered regional test on goat says shoulder, and nobody has shown
   otherwise."
5. **Kangaroo "consistency"** is stated by four vendors and one user report, all selling the
   material. It is almost certainly true (the fibre structure predicts it) but there is no
   independent measurement.
6. **Pigskin region vocabulary** barely exists in the sources found. The porcine file says so
   rather than inventing a nine-region map to match the bovine one.
